The first frost has come and gone, and the mill is settling in for the colder months. Here is what changes at Cobbleford Co-Working between now and spring.

## Holiday hours

We keep our usual hours through the season, with one exception: we close at noon on the last weekday before the holidays and reopen after the new year. Dedicated desk and private office members keep 24/7 key-fob access throughout.

## Staying warm

The heat pumps run a little warmer on the main floor in the mornings. If your corner feels cold, tell the front desk rather than bringing in a space heater - the old wiring is happier that way.

## Boots and bikes

There is a boot tray and a coat rack by the river entrance. The covered bike rack stays open all winter, but on snowy days you are welcome to bring your bike inside.
