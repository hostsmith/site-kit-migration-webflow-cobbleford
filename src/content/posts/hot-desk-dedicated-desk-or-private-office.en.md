Almost everyone who tours Cobbleford Co-Working asks the same question before they leave: which plan should I pick? The honest answer has less to do with your job title and more to do with how often you will come in, what you need to leave behind at night and how much of your day you spend on calls. Here is how we help new members decide.

## Hot Desk - {hotDeskPrice} a month

A hot desk gives you ten days a month on the main floor during business hours. Grab any open seat, plug in and get to work. It includes two hours of meeting room time, free coffee and tea, and an invitation to every member event.

**A good fit if** you work from home most of the week and want a change of scene, a dependable connection for important calls or simply some company. Freelancers, consultants and remote employees often start here.

## Dedicated Desk - {dedicatedDeskPrice} a month

A dedicated desk is yours alone, with a lockable drawer, a monitor and a chair adjusted just for you. You also get a personal locker, mail and package handling, six hours of meeting room time and 24/7 access.

**A good fit if** you come in three or more days a week and you are tired of packing up every evening. Rowan Pettibone, a freelance illustrator, moved from a hot desk to a dedicated desk by the window so the sketchbooks and paints could stay put.

## Private Office - {privateOfficePrice} a month

A private office is a lockable, furnished room for up to four people, ready on day one. It comes with twelve hours of meeting room time, a business mailing address and 24/7 access.

**A good fit if** you are a small team, you handle confidential client work or you spend much of the day on video calls. Quinn Haverford's nonprofit team moved into an office last spring and has room to grow without signing a long lease.

## Four questions to ask yourself

- **How many days a week will I really come in?** Fewer than three? Start with a hot desk - you can upgrade at any time.
- **Do I need to leave things here?** A second monitor, files or a favorite plant are much easier with a desk or an office of your own.
- **How much of my day is on calls?** The phone booths are open to every member, but if you are on video for hours, an office keeps you and your neighbors happy.
- **Do I need a business address?** Dedicated desks include mail and package handling, and private offices add a mailing address you can put on your website.

## Still not sure? Try it first

Every plan is month-to-month, so you can switch as your work changes. Nonprofits and students get a discount, and annual memberships get two months free when paid up front.
